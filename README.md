# 👋 Hi, I'm Pema Riczin

### UX / Product Designer • Design Technologist • Front-End Developer • AI Explorer

I’m a **UX / Product Designer and Design Technologist focused on creating human-centered digital products and technology solutions for social impact**.

My primary interest is working with **nonprofits, NGOs, charities, community organizations, and mission-driven teams** to design better ways for people to access services, connect with communities, participate, contribute, and create measurable impact.

I work across the full product journey:

**Research → Define → Ideate → Design → Prototype → Build → Test → Improve → Deploy**

I enjoy combining **UX research, design thinking, product strategy, front-end development, AI, APIs, data, and emerging technologies** to turn complex problems into useful digital experiences.

## 🌍 My Focus

**Social Impact • Nonprofit Technology • NGO Digital Transformation • Human-Centered Design**

I’m particularly interested in building solutions for:

* Nonprofits, NGOs, and charities
* Community development and engagement
* Education and learning
* Healthcare and public-service access
* Conservation and environmental initiatives
* Humanitarian and disaster-response systems
* Volunteer and donor engagement
* Social enterprises and ethical tourism
* AI-powered tools for mission-driven organizations
* Data, dashboards, mapping, and impact measurement

My goal is to use technology **with purpose**—supporting organizations and communities rather than building technology for technology’s sake.
